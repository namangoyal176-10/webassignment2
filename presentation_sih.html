01. Problem Statement & Proposed Solution

Addressing core administrative inefficiencies in university residential hostels

Slide 02 / 06
⚠️ The Existing Problem
  • Capacity Over-Allotment: Manual spreadsheets cause double-bookings, ghost reservations, and room overfills beyond legal fire safety capacities.
  • Unfair Static Dining Fees: Students are charged a fixed monthly mess fee regardless of academic leaves, semester breaks, or meal absences.
  • Unaccountable Maintenance: Complaints written in physical logbooks get delayed with zero SLA tracking, prioritization, or resident accountability.
  • Lack of Real-Time Analytics: Wardens cannot quickly visualize block-wise occupancy or room availability metrics during admissions.
💡 The Proposed Solution: CampusNest
  • Zero-Overbooking State Machine: Database-enforced capacity constraints guarantee occupiedBeds ≤ capacity at all times.
  • Dynamic Attendance-Based Mess Invoicing: Automated calculation using formula: Total = (Days Present × Daily Rate) + Maintenance.
  • End-to-End Maintenance Ticketing: Priority categorised ticketing (Urgent, High, Medium) with real-time status transitions and resolution remarks.
  • Warden KPI Dashboard: Real-time Chart.js interactive visualizations for block occupancy and bed distribution metrics.

03. Core Features & Implementation Methodology

Mathematical rigor, atomic database updates, and lifecycle management

Slide 04 / 06
🔒 Capacity Control
Bed allotment strictly validates occupiedBeds < capacity before approving assignments. Automated unit tests verify that 5th-student overflow attempts into a 4-capacity room are cleanly rejected.
Zero Overbooking
🔄 Atomic Room Transfers
When students transfer rooms, the system atomically decrements occupancy on the old room (re-opening the bed) and increments the new room occupancy in a single transaction sequence.
Atomic Concurrency
🍽️ Dining & Dynamic Bills
Full 7-day timetable with automatic current-day highlighting. Attendance aggregates generate transparent student invoices: (Days Present × Daily Rate) + Maintenance.
Consumption Billing
🛠️ Maintenance SLAs
Categorized tickets (Electrical, Plumbing, Wi-Fi, Furniture) with priority flags (Urgent, High, Medium). Wardens log resolution remarks with full audit timestamps.
Transparent SLA

04. Feasibility, Impact & Risk Analysis

Practical deployment metrics, quantitative benefits, and mitigation strategies

Slide 05 / 06
📈 Measurable Institutional Impact
  • 85% Reduction in Allocation Time: Automated digital application queues replace long physical queues during university admission weeks.
  • Zero Ghost Bed Allotments: Real-time bed counter prevents double-booking disputes.
  • 100% Financial Transparency: Itemized dining invoices eliminate disputes between students and mess caterers over leave deductions.
  • Zero Licensing Costs: Built exclusively on open-source web technologies (Node.js, MongoDB Atlas, Vercel), allowing frictionless adoption by public and private colleges.
🛡️ Challenges & Technical Mitigations
  • Challenge: Serverless Database Cold Starts
    Mitigation: Implemented global Mongoose promise caching in config/db.js with a 10-connection pool to preserve persistent sockets.
  • Challenge: Concurrency Spikes during Room Rush
    Mitigation: Pre-save schema locks and atomic conditional updates prevent race conditions.
  • Challenge: Unauthorized Access to Warden Controls
    Mitigation: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) middleware checks session credentials on every privileged route.
